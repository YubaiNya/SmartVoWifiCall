NewVoWiFi is a separate Android IMS integration developed for this user's device.
It bundles the existing SoftVoWiFi 0.3.48 arm64 engine as a binary dependency.
The original engine's supplied notices are preserved in licenses/.
The dependency's complete engine source is not part of this project.
The original SoftVoWiFi application is not replaced by this module; its installed
SIM provisioning helper is invoked to obtain current SIM identity on the device.
No SIM configuration, subscriber identifier, or runtime log is packaged here.
